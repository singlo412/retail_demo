## IMPACT ANALYSIS ON SUPPLY CHAIN OPERATIONS

The significant tariff increases taking effect in July 2025 will require strategic adjustments to our sourcing strategy. Key considerations include:

1. **China Impact:** The doubling of tariffs on Chinese imports will significantly affect cost structures across all categories. With China representing approximately 30% of our current manufacturing capacity, particularly in accessories and cosmetics, immediate diversification is recommended.

2. **European Premium Positioning:** Despite modest tariff increases, Italy, France, Portugal, and Spain remain strategically important for luxury categories due to craftsmanship and brand perception. The minimal tariff increases (generally under 3%) should be absorbed rather than shifting production away from these regions for premium products.

3. **Emerging Market Opportunities:** Vietnam, Cambodia, and Indonesia remain competitive even after tariff increases for mid-tier products, particularly in apparel and footwear categories. Consider increased capacity utilization in these markets.

4. **Category-Specific Strategies:**
   - **Leather Goods:** Italy and Spain remain preferred despite tariffs due to quality considerations
   - **Cosmetics:** Evaluate shifting production from China to France, despite higher production costs but lower comparative tariff impact
   - **Apparel:** Bangladesh and Vietnam offer significant cost advantages even with increased tariffs

5. **Long-Term Planning:** The unpredictable nature of these tariff changes suggests building more flexibility into our supply chain with multi-region capabilities for key product lines.

Recommended quarterly review of sourcing allocation by category to optimize for the changing tariff landscape while maintaining product integrity appropriate to our upscale positioning.
