# Convenience Store Weekly Management Guide: October - November 2025

## Week 1: October 1-7, 2025
**Seasonal Events:** Fall Season Beginning, World Smile Day (Oct 3)

**Sales Improvement Strategies:**
- **Promotions:**
  - Launch "Fall Favorites" promotion featuring seasonal coffees, teas, and apple cider
  - 2-for-1 on pumpkin-flavored snacks and beverages
  - Create fall value meal bundles (hot beverage + seasonal pastry)

- **Advertising/Displays:**
  - Set up "Fall Harvest" window displays with autumn colors
  - Place A-frame sign outside highlighting seasonal hot beverages
  - Create promotional counter cards for fall-themed items

- **Store Atmosphere:**
  - Begin transitioning to fall décor with subtle leaf garlands and warm-colored signage
  - Update playlist to include relaxing autumn-themed background music
  - Start displaying small Halloween merchandise in a dedicated section

- **Inventory Focus:**
  - Increase stock of comfort foods, hot beverage supplies, and grab-and-go warm meals
  - Begin bringing in early Halloween candy multi-packs for early shoppers

## Week 2: October 8-14, 2025
**Seasonal Events:** Columbus Day/Indigenous Peoples' Day (Oct 13)

**Sales Improvement Strategies:**
- **Promotions:**
  - Long weekend special offers tied to the Monday holiday
  - "Discover New Flavors" promotion highlighting international snacks and beverages
  - Launch loyalty card with double points on holiday Monday

- **Advertising/Displays:**
  - Create holiday weekend promotional endcap displays
  - Set up "Road Trip Essentials" display for holiday travelers
  - Hang promotional banners highlighting weekend deals

- **Store Atmosphere:**
  - Enhance fall decorations with more prominent autumn elements
  - Begin introducing Halloween-themed background music during peak hours
  - Add fall-scented air fresheners in store

- **Inventory Focus:**
  - Stock up on travel-sized necessities, car accessories, and road trip snacks
  - Increase inventory of ready-to-eat meals for holiday weekend
  - Begin expanding Halloween candy and decoration sections

## Week 3: October 15-21, 2025
**Seasonal Events:** National Boss's Day (Oct 16), World Food Day (Oct 16)

**Sales Improvement Strategies:**
- **Promotions:**
  - "Treat Your Boss" special on premium coffees and gourmet snacks
  - World Food Day sampling event featuring new international snacks
  - Mid-week happy hour promotion (4-6pm) on coffee and pastries

- **Advertising/Displays:**
  - Display "Perfect Gift for Boss's Day" suggestions near register
  - Create food-themed displays highlighting global snack options
  - Set up Halloween costume accessory display (face paint, masks, etc.)

- **Store Atmosphere:**
  - Increase Halloween décor to 50% of seasonal displays
  - Add small pumpkins and gourds to checkout counters
  - Schedule staff to wear Halloween accessories during shifts

- **Inventory Focus:**
  - Stock greeting cards and small gift items for Boss's Day
  - Ensure full inventory of Halloween candy varieties
  - Begin bringing in baking supplies for upcoming holiday season

## Week 4: October 22-28, 2025
**Seasonal Events:** Halloween Season Intensifies

**Sales Improvement Strategies:**
- **Promotions:**
  - Launch "Halloween Countdown" deals (different special each day)
  - Introduce limited-time spooky-themed coffee drinks and snacks
  - Start "Mix & Match" candy promotions for party planners

- **Advertising/Displays:**
  - Convert main window display to full Halloween theme
  - Set up dedicated costume accessory and last-minute Halloween needs section
  - Place promotional standees for Halloween movie theater candy bundles

- **Store Atmosphere:**
  - Complete transition to Halloween décor throughout store
  - Play Halloween-themed music and occasional spooky sound effects
  - Staff to wear Halloween-themed uniforms or accessories

- **Inventory Focus:**
  - Maximum stock levels of all Halloween candy and party supplies
  - Increase inventory of last-minute costume accessories and makeup
  - Stock party ice, beverages, and quick party foods

## Week 5: October 29-November 4, 2025
**Seasonal Events:** Halloween (Oct 31), Daylight Saving Time Ends (Nov 2)

**Sales Improvement Strategies:**
- **Promotions:**
  - Last-minute Halloween essentials bundle deals
  - November 1st candy clearance sale
  - "Fall Back" promotion for Daylight Saving (energy drinks, coffee)

- **Advertising/Displays:**
  - Halloween night emergency supplies endcap (batteries, flashlights, etc.)

- **Store Atmosphere:**
  - Peak Halloween décor and atmosphere through October 31
  - Swift transition to harvest/thanksgiving themes on November 1
  - Adjust opening procedures for time change (extra coffee samples)

- **Inventory Focus:**
  - Monitor Halloween inventory for last-minute replenishment
  - Prepare markdown strategy for Halloween items on November 1
  - Begin increasing stock of Thanksgiving-related items

## Week 9: November 26-30, 2025
**Seasonal Events:** Thanksgiving (Nov 27), Black Friday (Nov 28), Small Business Saturday (Nov 29)

**Sales Improvement Strategies:**
- **Promotions:**
  - Thanksgiving Day convenience items special pricing
  - Black Friday doorbusters and all-day deals
  - Small Business Saturday local product spotlights and specials

- **Advertising/Displays:**
  - Thanksgiving Day essentials display (ice, beverages, batteries)
  - Bold Black Friday signage throughout store
  - Create holiday shopping season launch displays

- **Store Atmosphere:**
  - Begin transition to holiday/Christmas décor on Black Friday
  - Full holiday music playlist implementation
  - Staff to wear holiday-themed accessories starting Black Friday

- **Inventory Focus:**
  - Stock convenience items for Thanksgiving gatherings
  - Prepare high-volume Black Friday inventory of popular items
  - Begin major holiday season inventory deployment

**Additional Notes:**
- Ensure staffing levels match expected customer traffic, particularly around Halloween and Thanksgiving
- Monitor inventory daily during peak seasonal periods
- Coordinate with suppliers for quick replenishment of seasonal items
- Adjust store hours as needed to accommodate holiday shopping patterns
