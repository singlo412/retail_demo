
## October 1 - November 30, 2025
### Executive Overview for Upscale Retail Women's Fashion

---

## WEEK 1: OCTOBER 1-7, 2025
**SEASONAL HIGHLIGHTS: Fall Fashion Peak, Early Fall Luxury Showcase**

### Merchandising Strategy:
- Transition main floor displays to showcase rich autumn colors - burgundy, forest green, camel, and amber tones
- Feature cashmere sweaters, premium denim, and leather jackets at store entrance points
- Install new "Fall Luxe Collection" signage with gold accents matching our brand guidelines

### Promotional Activities:
- Launch "October Style Edit" lookbook to VIP customers (email October 1, physical mailer by October 3)
- Offer complimentary style consultation with purchase of $500+ (promote through store signage)
- Introduce limited-edition fall accessories collection with designer collaboration

### Visual & Experiential Elements:
- Install autumn-inspired window displays with preserved maple leaves, amber lighting, and warm textures
- Refresh in-store scent to warm spiced amber fragrance
- Update playlist to include sophisticated acoustic covers and jazz standards that evoke autumn mood

### Staff Focus:
- Monday morning meeting: Train team on styling fall layering techniques with our luxury basics
- Ensure every associate can speak to the craftsmanship of our Italian leather goods and cashmere pieces
- Role play premium customer interactions focused on building fall capsule wardrobes
