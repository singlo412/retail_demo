# Weekly Strategic Plan for Women's Department
## December 2025 - January 2026

---

## WEEK 1: December 1-7, 2025

**Seasonal Events:** Cyber Monday (Dec 1), Early Holiday Shopping Season

**Strategic Focus:** Transition from online promotions to in-store holiday shopping experience

**Action Items:**
- **Monday (Cyber Monday):** Cross-promote in-store exclusives that aren't available online to drive foot traffic after online shopping
- Unveil holiday window displays featuring winter luxury ensembles with cashmere, silk, and fine wool pieces
- Install primary Christmas decor with sophisticated color scheme (champagne, silver, and deep burgundy)
- Begin "12 Days of Gifting" campaign highlighting curated gift selections at different price points
- Position resort wear collection at store entrance for clients planning winter getaways
- **Visual Merchandising:** Create three main holiday vignettes focusing on "Evening Soiree," "Winter Getaway," and "Luxe Lounging"
- **Music:** Introduce subtle holiday instrumental playlist (no traditional carols yet, focus on sophisticated ambient music)
- **Staff Training:** Conduct refresher on gift services including complimentary gift wrapping, personal shopping, and corporate gifting options

**Promotion:** Launch "Holiday Wish List" service with personalized stylist consultations and notification service for gift-givers

---

## WEEK 2: December 8-14, 2025

**Seasonal Events:** Peak holiday shopping period

**Strategic Focus:** Gift-giving guidance and premium shopping experience

**Action Items:**
- Release holiday gift guide categorized by recipient (mother, sister, friend) with styling suggestions
- Introduce "Sip & Shop" evenings (Thursday-Saturday) with complimentary champagne and petit fours from 5-8pm
- **Visual Merchandising:** Rotate showcase displays to highlight statement pieces, focusing on special occasion wear
- Create "Last-Minute Gift" section with pre-wrapped luxury accessories and giftable items
- **Decorations:** Enhance holiday decor with fresh floral arrangements featuring white amaryllis and evergreen accents
- **Music:** Transition to contemporary holiday instrumentals with increased tempo during peak shopping hours
- **Staff Focus:** Implement "Gift Concierge" stations at strategic store locations to assist with gift selections
- Display winter accessories (cashmere scarves, leather gloves) prominently near the entrance as add-on purchases

**Promotion:** "Gifting Made Luxurious" – Spend $500 on gifts, receive $75 gift card for January redemption

---

## WEEK 3: December 15-21, 2025

**Seasonal Events:** Winter Solstice (Dec 21), Last full shopping week before Christmas

**Strategic Focus:** Create sense of urgency while maintaining luxury experience

**Action Items:**
- Introduce extended shopping hours (9am-9pm) with early morning "Power Shopping" sessions featuring personal shoppers by appointment
- **Visual Merchandising:** Highlight evening wear and cocktail attire for holiday parties
- Create "Winter Solstice" themed display with winter whites, ice blue, and silver pieces from the collection
- **Decoration Update:** Add subtle lighting enhancements to create warm, inviting atmosphere during extended evening hours
- **Music:** Rotate to upbeat holiday mix during day, transitioning to sophisticated lounge covers of holiday classics in evening
- Offer complimentary gift wrapping station with signature packaging
- Position impulse purchase items (fine jewelry, fragrances) near checkout areas
- Implement "Complete the Look" merchandising strategy with coordinated separates

**Promotion:** "Winter Solstice Soiree" – Special evening event on Dec 21 with exclusive previews of Resort collection and complimentary styling services

---

## WEEK 4: December 22-28, 2025

**Seasonal Events:** Christmas Eve (Dec 24), Christmas Day (Dec 25), Boxing Day (Dec 26)

**Strategic Focus:** Last-minute luxury shopping and transition to post-holiday sales

**Action Items:**
- **Dec 22-23:** Emphasize expedited delivery options and in-store pickup for online orders
- **Dec 24 (Christmas Eve):** Special hours (9am-5pm) with "SOS Gift Service" offering instant styling advice and gift solutions
- **Dec 25:** CLOSED
- **Dec 26-28:** Begin selective markdowns (avoid using "sale" terminology, instead use "Winter Collection Adjustment")
- **Visual Merchandising:** Transition from holiday to winter luxury theme, featuring cold-weather luxe cashmere and outerwear
- **Decoration:** Begin subtle removal of overtly Christmas elements while maintaining elegant winter atmosphere
- **Music:** Return to standard store playlist with occasional winter-themed selections
- Focus on gift card redemption strategies with "How to Style Your Gift" lookbooks

**Promotion:** "Winter Investment Pieces" campaign highlighting timeless luxury items at adjusted prices (Boxing Day through end of week)

---

## WEEK 5: December 29, 2025 - January 4, 2026

**Seasonal Events:** New Year's Eve (Dec 31), New Year's Day (Jan 1)

**Strategic Focus:** Year-end inventory management and New Year refreshment

**Action Items:**
- **Dec 29-30:** Display prominent "Last Chance 2025" signage for year-end tax benefits on major purchases
- **Dec 31:** Close early (5pm) with special styling appointments for last-minute New Year's Eve outfits
- **Jan 1:** CLOSED
- **Jan 2-4:** Launch "New Year, New Wardrobe" campaign featuring transitional winter-to-spring pieces
- **Visual Merchandising:** Create aspirational displays focusing on "2026 Style Resolutions" with sophisticated workwear and versatile pieces
- **Decorations:** Remove all holiday elements, refresh with clean, minimalist displays featuring metallic accents
- **Music:** Energetic, forward-looking playlist with contemporary instrumental selections
- Strategically place fitness and wellness-related apparel to capture resolution-minded customers

**Promotion:** "New Year Refresh" – Purchase $300 in new arrivals, receive complimentary wardrobe consultation and closet organization tips

---

## WEEK 6: January 5-11, 2026

**Seasonal Events:** Back to work/routine season

**Strategic Focus:** Workwear refresh and wellness-centered fashion

**Action Items:**
- Launch "Executive Style" campaign highlighting professional attire for the new year
- Create capsule wardrobe displays showing how 10 key pieces can create 30 different looks
- **Visual Merchandising:** Focus on sophisticated layering techniques with luxe basics and statement accessories
- **Decoration:** Bright, clean aesthetic with emphasis on organization and simplicity
- **Music:** Upbeat yet sophisticated background music to energize shoppers
- Introduce "Mindful Shopping" concept with quality-focused messaging about investment dressing
- Host weekday early evening "Career Wardrobe Workshop" events with styling tips for professional advancement

**Promotion:** "Workwear Upgrade" – Purchase three or more workwear pieces and receive 15% adjustment and complimentary alterations

---

## WEEK 7: January 12-18, 2026

**Seasonal Events:** Deep winter season

**Strategic Focus:** Winter luxury and early resort wear

**Action Items:**
- Create dual focus: cold weather essentials and early vacation/resort wear
- **Visual Merchandising:** Split store focus with winter luxe (cashmere, wool, leather) in main areas and resort collection (cruise wear, swimwear) in dedicated section
- **Decorations:** Incorporate subtle tropical elements in resort section while maintaining sophisticated winter atmosphere elsewhere
- **Music:** Standard playlist with geographical zoning (upbeat in resort section, sophisticated in winter section)
- Host "Winter Wellness" event featuring luxurious athleisure and activewear styling session with local fitness experts
- Feature "Investment Pieces" highlighting timeless luxury items that retain value

**Promotion:** "Winter Escape" – Special bundled pricing on resort wear collections and tropical accessories

---

## WEEK 8: January 19-25, 2026

**Seasonal Events:** Martin Luther King Jr. Day (Jan 17), Deep winter clearance traditionally begins

**Strategic Focus:** Strategic inventory reduction while maintaining brand positioning

**Action Items:**
- Begin significant winter inventory adjustments ("Winter Collection Realignment")
- **Visual Merchandising:** Create "Last of Season" displays for winter items while showcasing early spring arrivals in key areas
- **Decorations:** Introduce elements of spring (subtle floral arrangements) while maintaining winter atmosphere
- **Music:** Standard store playlist with moderate tempo
- Train staff on "Last Opportunity" vs "Sale" language to maintain luxury positioning
- Create lookbooks pairing discounted winter items with full-price new arrivals
- Host "Spring Preview" VIP event for loyalty customers with exclusive first access to new collections

**Promotion:** "Winter Investment" – Tiered gift card rewards based on winter collection purchases ($500=$50, $1000=$150, $2000=$400)

---

## WEEK 9: January 26-31, 2026

**Seasonal Events:** Transition to February (Valentine's Day preparation begins)

**Strategic Focus:** Final winter clearance and early spring positioning

**Action Items:**
- Move all remaining winter merchandise to designated area with significant adjustments
- **Visual Merchandising:** Primary focus on new spring arrivals and Valentine's Day gift selections
- Create prominent displays featuring transitional pieces that work now through early spring
- **Decorations:** Begin Valentine's theme with sophisticated interpretation (subtle rose golds, blush tones)
- **Music:** Introduce more energetic, fresh playlist signaling seasonal change
- Position new spring accessories prominently for immediate purchase satisfaction
- Begin training on spring collection highlights and coming trends
- Launch "Spring 2026 Trend Report" highlighting key seasonal investments

**Promotion:** "Valentine's Preview" – Special curated gift selections with complimentary gift packaging and early bird pricing for pre-orders

---

*Note: All promotional activities should be executed with sophisticated, elevated language and presentation consistent with our upscale brand positioning. Avoid discount-focused terminology in customer-facing communications.*
