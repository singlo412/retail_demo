# Convenience Store Manager's Weekly Guide: December 2025 - January 2026

## Week 1: December 1-7, 2025

**Seasonal Events:**
- Early December holiday shopping
- St. Nicholas Day (December 6)
- Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day (December 7)

**Strategy Focus: Holiday Kick-off**

**Tasks:**
1. **Front Display Setup:**
   - Create a holiday entrance display with red and green colors
   - Feature holiday snacks and seasonal beverages at the front

2. **Promotions:**
   - "Holiday Season Kickoff" - 2 for $5 on hot chocolate mixes and holiday cookies
   - Introduce holiday gift card display near registers with $5 bonus on $50 purchases

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Window posters announcing December deals and extended holiday hours
   - Position St. Nicholas Day themed candy/chocolate display (boots or stockings with treats)

4. **Ambiance:**
   - Begin playing holiday music (25% of playlist)
   - Install simple string lights around store perimeter

5. **Staff Focus:**
   - Brief team on upcoming holiday promotions
   - Distribute holiday-themed name badges

## Week 2: December 8-14, 2025

**Seasonal Events:**
- Holiday shopping intensifies
- Hanukkah begins (December 14)

**Strategy Focus: Gift Solutions & Hanukkah Preparation**

**Tasks:**
1. **Special Displays:**
   - Set up "Last-Minute Gift" section with pre-wrapped items
   - Create dedicated Hanukkah display with traditional items (gelt, dreidels, blue/silver decorations)

2. **Promotions:**
   - "Grab & Go Gifts" bundle deals - discounted combinations of gift cards, candy boxes
   - Hanukkah special: Discounted price on candles and traditional treats

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Hang Hanukkah banner in visible location
   - Place "Gift Ideas Under $20" signs throughout store

4. **Ambiance:**
   - Increase holiday music to 50% of playlist
   - Add blue and silver decorations alongside red and green

5. **Digital Strategy:**
   - Schedule social media posts highlighting Hanukkah products
   - Email newsletter featuring gift card promotions and extended hours

## Week 3: December 15-21, 2025

**Seasonal Events:**
- Hanukkah continues
- Last full week before Christmas
- Winter Solstice (December 21)

**Strategy Focus: Last-Week Shopping Rush**

**Tasks:**
1. **Stock Management:**
   - Ensure full inventory of gift wrap, tape, cards, and batteries
   - Create "Forgotten Items" endcap with essentials for holiday meals and gifts

2. **Promotions:**
   - "Holiday Countdown" daily deals on different categories each day
   - Winter Solstice special: Hot beverages BOGO after 4pm on Dec 21

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Countertop displays for impulse gift purchases
   - Directional floor signs guiding customers to gift supplies, snacks, and beverages

4. **Ambiance:**
   - Increase holiday music to 75% of playlist
   - Staff wears holiday accessories (pins, hats, etc.)

5. **Extended Services:**
   - Offer free gift wrapping with $15+ purchase
   - Implement express checkout for 5 items or less

## Week 4: December 22-28, 2025

**Seasonal Events:**
- Christmas Eve (December 24)
- Christmas Day (December 25)
- Boxing Day (December 26)
- Kwanzaa begins (December 26)

**Strategy Focus: Holiday Peak & Last-Minute Essentials**

**Tasks:**
1. **Special Hours & Staffing:**
   - Extended hours December 22-24
   - Adjust holiday hours for Christmas Day
   - Create clear signage for holiday hours

2. **Promotions:**
   - Christmas Eve "Last Dash" deals on forgotten items (batteries, tape, etc.)
   - After-Christmas clearance begins Dec 26 (50% off holiday items)
   - Kwanzaa display with special pricing on relevant items

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Ensure Kwanzaa display is visible with appropriate colors and information
   - Convert front display to "Holiday Essentials" (batteries, film, memory cards, etc.)

4. **Inventory Management:**
   - Begin rotating in New Year's products mid-week
   - Mark down remaining Christmas-specific merchandise

5. **Customer Experience:**
   - Prepare small complimentary candy canes/treats for customers
   - Have staff wish customers appropriate holiday greetings

## Week 5: December 29, 2025 - January 4, 2026

**Seasonal Events:**
- New Year's Eve (December 31)
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Kwanzaa ends (January 1)

**Strategy Focus: New Year Celebration & Recovery**

**Tasks:**
1. **New Year's Display Setup:**
   - Front display featuring party supplies and celebration essentials
   - Secondary display with recovery items (pain relievers, sports drinks, etc.)

2. **Promotions:**
   - New Year's Eve party package deals (cups, mixers, snacks)
   - January 1-2: "Recovery Day" specials on sports drinks, ready-to-eat meals

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - "New Year, New You" signage promoting healthy snacks and beverages
   - Continue holiday clearance with increased discounts (70% off)

4. **Ambiance:**
   - Replace holiday music with upbeat contemporary playlist
   - Add New Year-themed decorations (sparkly decorations, "2026" signage)

5. **Staff Planning:**
   - Ensure adequate staffing for New Year's Eve rush
   - Begin planning January promotion calendar

## Week 6: January 5-11, 2026

**Seasonal Events:**
- Back to work/school period
- Post-holiday routine returns

**Strategy Focus: Healthy Start & Winter Necessities**

**Tasks:**
1. **Display Reset:**
   - Convert main displays to "Healthy Start" theme
   - Create prominent cold/flu remedies section

2. **Promotions:**
   - "New Year, New You" deals on healthy snacks, waters, protein bars
   - Bundle deals on coffee and breakfast items for returning commuters

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Window signs promoting immune boosters and winter wellness
   - Coupon booklet for January savings

4. **Ambiance:**
   - Remove remaining holiday decorations
   - Refresh store with clean, organized look

5. **Product Focus:**
   - Highlight comfort foods and warm beverages
   - Place winter car supplies (windshield fluid, ice scrapers) in visible location

## Week 7: January 12-18, 2026

**Seasonal Events:**
- Mid-winter period
- Lead-up to Martin Luther King Jr. Day weekend

**Strategy Focus: Winter Comfort & Weekend Preparation**

**Tasks:**
1. **Special Displays:**
   - "Winter Movie Night" bundles with snacks and beverages
   - Early Valentine's Day teaser display (small selection of cards/candy)

2. **Promotions:**
   - Hot food specials during lunch hours
   - Weekend preparation deals for upcoming MLK holiday weekend

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Promote loyalty program to boost post-holiday spending
   - Highlight winter car/home essentials with "Winter Ready" signage

4. **Digital Strategy:**
   - Social media push for weekend specials
   - Email promotion for coffee loyalty program

5. **Staff Focus:**
   - Train on upcoming Valentine's promotions
   - Review inventory needs for upcoming holiday weekend

## Week 8: January 19-25, 2026

**Seasonal Events:**
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day (January 19)
- Deep winter season

**Strategy Focus: Long Weekend & Valentine's Preparation**

**Tasks:**
1. **Holiday Weekend Prep:**
   - Create MLK Day weekend specials on snacks and ready-to-eat meals
   - Ensure adequate staffing for holiday foot traffic

2. **Valentine's Rollout:**
   - Expand Valentine's Day display with cards, chocolates, and gifts
   - Create special display for "Valentine's gifts under $10"

3. **Promotions:**
   - Launch coffee stamp card promotion (buy 7, get 1 free)
   - "Winter Blues Buster" deals on comfort foods and entertainment items

4. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Begin promoting early Valentine's shopping
   - Winter essentials endcap with marked-down prices

5. **Inventory Management:**
   - Clear remaining holiday merchandise with deep discounts
   - Begin receiving Valentine's Day inventory

## Week 9: January 26-31, 2026

**Seasonal Events:**
- Month-end
- Valentine's Day approaching (two weeks away)

**Strategy Focus: Valentine's Push & February Preparation**

**Tasks:**
1. **Major Display Conversion:**
   - Front of store dedicated to Valentine's Day merchandise
   - Secondary display featuring Super Bowl party supplies

2. **Promotions:**
   - "Early Bird Valentine's" special pricing for early shoppers
   - Month-end clearance on winter items

3. **Advertising/Placement:**
   - Window display updated to Valentine's theme
   - Countertop displays for impulse Valentine's purchases

4. **Planning:**
   - Finalize February promotional calendar
   - Order inventory for Super Bowl (February 8) and Valentine's Day

5. **Customer Engagement:**
   - Valentine's-themed sampling events (chocolate, candy)
   - Launch "Share the Love" social media contest

---

**Additional Notes:**
- Adjust all strategies based on weather conditions and local events
- Monitor inventory levels daily during holiday weeks
- Track promotion effectiveness and be ready to pivot strategies if needed
- Maintain photo documentation of successful displays for future reference
